# Initial Concept
Vitality is a comprehensive hospital management dashboard designed to provide real-time visibility into operations and facilitate data-driven decision-making for hospital administrators and medical staff.

# Product Definition

## Target Users
- **Hospital Administrators:** Focused on high-level monitoring of bed availability, staffing levels, and overall facility efficiency.
- **Medical Staff and Nurses:** Focused on real-time patient flow management, active alerts, and immediate operational needs.

## Goals
- **Real-Time Visualization:** Provide an intuitive and accurate view of current hospital status and patient movement.
- **Scenario Simulation:** Enable analysts and administrators to simulate chaotic events or policy changes to analyze potential impacts before implementation.
- **Operational Efficiency:** Streamline decision-making by consolidating disparate data into a unified, alert-driven interface.
- **Supervised Intelligence (HITL):** A strict Human-in-the-Loop state machine for high-impact decisions, ensuring clinical oversight for automated distress signals.

## Key Features
- **Live Dashboards:** Interactive charts and visualizations for Patient Flow and Hospital Status.
- **Simulation Engine:** Advanced controls for chaos engineering, allowing users to stress-test hospital operations and perform live impact analysis.
- **Unified Alerting System:** Centralized management of operational alerts to ensure rapid response to bottlenecks or emergencies.
- **Policy-as-Code Engine:** Dynamic, database-backed configuration of triage parameters (boosts, timeouts, weights) allowing real-time tuning of system behavior.
- **Secure Access:** Role-aware authentication ensuring personalized views and secure access to hospital data.

## Value Proposition
Vitality solves critical hospital management challenges by reducing patient wait times and bottlenecks through real-time monitoring, optimizing resource allocation during emergencies, and providing a single source of truth for complex hospital data systems.
